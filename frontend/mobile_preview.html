The Bigger Picture
A recurring pattern of thinking about how fragmented, siloed data in India prevents systems-level research — and how lightweight, community-driven collection can democratise what has historically required institutional gatekeepers.
Super Idea
A federated open data infrastructure for India — aggregating agricultural satellite imagery, oral economic histories, and urban microclimate readings — enabling cross-domain research currently blocked by data fragmentation and institutional access barriers.

How They Differ
Each idea operates in a different domain and uses a different collection mechanism. None contains the other — satellite imagery cannot capture oral economic knowledge, and neither captures urban microclimate readings.
Relationship
complementary
Why These Connect
These are not variations of the same idea — each brings a distinct data modality. Together they reveal a coherent design philosophy: lightweight participatory collection as an alternative to centralised institutional data production.
